Responsive design — department view

  • Same shell as admin: bottom bar with Apps · Edition · Settings · Sign out, persistent on every screen
  • Apps menu: the same 4 priority apps up front — department's "… other" only has Calendar and Budget (no Journal, Invoices, Cost Centers, Users, Dashboard)
  • No approve/reject or "record in journal" actions on Expense Reports — a department user submits and tracks status, they don't review
  • No event/shift management controls (assign staff, delete shift, delete event) — sign up / withdraw only
  • Budget is read-only here — planned vs. actual per line, no editing
  • Every tap target is 44px or larger
